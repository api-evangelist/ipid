Scam losses in Singapore continue to rise, with rental, investment, and impersonation scams costing millions. Despite existing anti-scam measures, fraudsters exploit gaps in payment verification. Learn how Verification of Payee (VoP) and stronger payee validation can break the cycle of trust scammers rely on.
