# iPiD (International Payments Identity)

> iPiD provides real-time Know Your Payee (KYP) verification for cross-border and domestic payments. Its single, payment-rail-agnostic API lets banks, PSPs, and corporates validate bank account ownership and match payee names before funds move, reducing authorized push payment (APP) fraud, business email compromise, and failed transactions. Founded 2021; backed by QED Investors and others. This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public site and catalog metadata; iPiD does not publish a machine-readable API specification publicly (API access is gated behind a sales/demo process).

## Products
- [iPiD Validate](https://ipid.tech/global-validation): Global payee / bank account verification with name-matching (no-match / close-match / strong-match indicators).
- [iPiD Node](https://ipid.tech/solutions): Scheme-compliance layer for EU Verification of Payee (VoP), UK Confirmation of Payee (CoP), FATF Recommendation 16, and US Nacha.
- Payment Agent: An agentic payments verification layer.
